# Applause · emids R&R — MASTER PROMPT (single paste, friend-deck structure + our rules)

Design a premium, modern AI-powered Employee Rewards & Recognition (R&R) Portal for a large enterprise — a blend of LinkedIn-style social recognition, a premium fintech rewards wallet, and an AI productivity workspace, not a traditional HR portal.

Design direction: minimal, sophisticated, enterprise-grade UI — premium Apple/Tesla-inspired aesthetic. Clean white/light-gray surfaces, dark charcoal typography (#0E0E0E ink), subtle borders (#E4E4E2 1px), soft shadows, generous whitespace, rounded cards (14–16px), refined micro-interactions, subtle gradients and tasteful glassmorphism only where appropriate, Inter-style sans-serif. Brand palette (exact hexes — use these): Emids Teal #47A2B0 = primary accent, present on every surface at ≥20% (buttons, links, active nav chip, chart strokes). Warm White #F2F2F0 for cards/sheets/app surfaces. Signals only, never decorative: Surgeon Green #45BDA0 (success), Sterile Blue #00B9F9 (info), Signal Red #E04F4F (alert/error). Human accents: Mauve #B89DCB + Yellow #F2C94C — one per photo, max two per chart. Avoid clutter, generic HR-dashboard styling, and overly playful gamification.

System stage: a full-bleed soft gradient from Teal to Emids Black; the entire app floats as ONE Warm White card centered on the stage (24px radius, soft shadow).

Data reality (use these names, no lorem ipsum): Prastina is the org head; Shan (senior manager, L2) and Prateesh (manager, L1, budget owner) sit on the SAME level beneath Prastina; interns Nikhilesh, Syed, Shourya report to Prateesh. There is also a generic "Awards office" (Admin/HR). Demo wallet belongs to Nikhilesh: 2,450 points.

SEVEN-PERSONA SYSTEM: an avatar chip in the top-right opens a drawer of 7 personas, each with avatar + one-liner — 1 Employee (Nikhilesh, receives awards, spends points) · 2 Employee (Shourya, second employee view) · 3 Peer Employee (Syed, peer kudos with ≤100-pt attach) · 4 Manager L1 (Prateesh, three interns, carries the Manager-boost quota) · 5 Manager L2 (Shan, carries Quarterly Excellence quota) · 6 Manager L3 (Prastina, org head, carries Annual Pride quota) · 7 Admin/HR (Awards office, rules/budgets/ballot/jury). Switching the chip re-visions the home screen per persona and reveals manager tools only for managers. This persona switcher is the core hook of the demo — make it delightful.

AWARD RULES REGISTRY (the product's spine): an Admin/HR screen "Award rules — the quota registry", a six-row table: Award (icon + name + points) · Given by · Approval route as small chips · Quota carrier (editable dropdown: Prateesh·L1 / Shan·L2 / Prastina·L3 / Awards office) · Carrier pool remaining with thin progress bar. Rows: Kudos thanks (free, anyone, no approval) · Peer reward ≤100 (cap check, awards-office pool) · Manager boost 100–1,000 (pool guard 10,000/mo, Prateesh) · Quarterly Excellence 2,500 (L1 draft → L2 approval → deduct, Shan) · Annual Pride 10,000 (L2 → L3, Prastina) · Employee of the Quarter 5,000 (AI review → L1 → L2 → L3 → office → jury, awards office). Note card: "The quota-carrying manager is not one fixed person — each award type names its own. Change a carrier and every give/approval flow re-routes instantly." Show a toast after a change ("Quarterly Excellence: carrier changed Shan → Prastina · flows re-routed ⚖"). Budget pools: Prateesh 10,000/month, Shan 25,000, Prastina 50,000, office 40,000; exceeding a pool blocks the gift offering "reduce" or "request more budget" (escalates Shan → Prastina).

Main Employee Dashboard — personalized "Good morning, Nikhilesh!" command center: recognition feed (avatar/name/department/message, category badge, like·celebrate·comment·share with Mauve hearts, highlighted kudos-of-the-week card, "＋ Give Recognition" primary CTA); points wallet card (2,450 pts balance, earned this quarter, spent, recent transactions, big Redem CTA, progress ring toward next milestone); quick actions (Give praise · Nominate for award · Redeem points · My recognitions · View team); recognition insights (received 18, given 12, score 64%, team trends); upcoming (EoQ deadline, pending nominations/approvals, reminders). The home feed shows a manager-boost post with a Yellow "+500 boost pts" chip.

Recognition experience: a 3-step wizard modal (Category → Message → Preview) completable in 30 seconds — recipient autocomplete, award type, points presets 25/50/100 chips (peer cap 100; kudos free, "Recognition and money are separate by design"), message with LIVE AI checks ("Names a real contribution ✓", "Positive tone ✓", "Recipient matches the directory ✓"; red states for vague text or non-directory recipient), preview with routing strip "This award's budget is carried by Prateesh (Manager L1) — per Award rules" as path chips You → Prateesh → pool deduct, then smooth send + professional success state "Recognition sent ✨" (subtle confetti, not an arcade).

Route the approvals automatically: an intern-nominated EoQ walks the tree Prateesh → Shan → Prastina → Awards office (manager nominations start at Shan); approval view = vertical timeline with per-step states (approved green dot / in-progress teal ring / waiting grey), action bar visible ONLY to the current step's persona ("Approve (as Prateesh)"), Request changes/Reject for L1, and peer endorsements instead of duplicate nominations (+6 peers chip). Above the timeline: "Payout 5,000 pts from the awards office pool".

Rewards marketplace: premium e-commerce feel — dark "THRIVE REWARDS · Use your points" hero with persistent wallet chip, category tabs (All cards / Experiences / Memberships / Gifting), featured "A weekend to remember" banner, voucher grid (₹2,000 digital gift card, ₹2,250 travel credit, 3-month premium 1,650, ₹2,600 annual membership), every card shows Reward → Brand → Points ("2,000 pts · ₹200 credit", 10 points = ₹1) with Mauve wishlist heart, Redeem button or "Need 550 more" ghost. Redemption = 2–3 step flow (select → confirm balance −cost → success with voucher code "APPL-8F3K-47 · code by email & wallet").

Manager dashboard (personas L1–L3): team recognition activity, recently recognized, pending nominations/approvals (badge 2), team rewards budget card per carried pool ("Prateesh · L1 pool — 3,500 remaining" with bar and chip "⚡ boost", "Request more budget" with tooltip "Escalates to Shan, then Prastina"), recognition trends and distribution, quarterly nominations; clear actions Approve · Endorse · Nominate · Recognize · View Team. Charts sparing, insights actionable.

Company tree: elegant interactive org chart on a dotted canvas with teal connective lines and smooth expand/collapse; search with ⌘K; clickable nodes (avatar, name, one-liner) opening a recognition profile; notes "The tree decides routing" and "Joins/exits re-route next morning". Structure per the Data reality above — Prastina top, Shan & Prateesh same level, interns bracketed under Prateesh.

End-of-Quarter awards: executive jury space — candidates with eligibility tags, AI-generated nomination summary, per-candidate stats chips (Innovation 9 · Leadership impact 4 · $1.2M revenue protected), YOUR SCORECARD with three criteria rows (Reported uplift to leadership / Value demonstration / Culture of inclusion), each a 1–10 round-dot selector filling in teal, optional note, Submit scorecard; voting table (recognitions, impact stars, AI check ✓, endorsements), Declare winner flow, winner overlay with medal (Yellow ring) and "+5,000 points posted to the wallet ledger".

AI Recognition Copilot: persistent copilot in the navigation ("Ask AI" pill) opening a modern copilot panel — helps write recognition messages, validates nominations (five check rows animating to green ticks: eligible employee · names work+impact · evidence found · no duplicate · policy compliance), checks policy, detects duplicates, summarizes nominations into a jury draft, routes approvals, explains approval status, suggests award categories, generates manager digests. Show reasoning as concise progress cards with typing/thinking states. Example: "Help me nominate Nikhilesh for Customer Champion" → "Based on Nikhilesh's recent recognitions and achievements, he meets the eligibility criteria — I've prepared a draft nomination."

Notifications center: grouped + prioritized — recognized you, manager approval required, nomination approved, award deadline approaching, points received, redemption successful, AI workflow updates, budget-request escalations.

Employee profile: photo, role/department, recognition received/given, badges, awards, recognition timeline, current points balance — professional and privacy-conscious.

Navigation: left rail Home · Recognize · Awards · Jury · Award rules · My wallet · Marketplace · People; top strip with search (⌘K), bell with badge, "Ask AI" pill, persona chip. For managers dynamically surface Team · Approvals · Budget.

Motion: smooth page transitions, animated card entrance, hover elevation, recognition reaction animation with light confetti on send, animated points balance on earn/redeem, smooth redemption transitions, animated org-tree expansion, skeleton loading states, AI thinking states, approval progress animation, toasts, elegant modals — premium, fast, intentional.

Responsive: desktop-first, clean translation to mobile prioritizing Give Recognition → get recognized → wallet → redeem → approve.

Connected high-fidelity prototype journey: Home → Give Recognition → Recognition sent ✨ → recipient notification → wallet updates → marketplace → select voucher → redeem → switch to Manager L1 → approval → EoQ jury scoresheet → declare winner → Award rules (flip Quarterly carrier) → AI copilot. Closing line of the experience: "Recognition is effortless. Rewards are instant. AI handles the complexity."
